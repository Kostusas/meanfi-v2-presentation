Defaults and capabilities

result = meanfi.solver(model, guess)
03
Adaptive k-integration

Refine where the integrand is difficult.

Uniform grids spend points everywhere. Adaptive integration concentrates work near local features such as Fermi surfaces, van Hove features, crossings, and nodal lines.

Zero temperature AdaptiveSimplex refines a simplicial partition of the Brillouin zone.
Finite temperature AdaptiveQuadrature places cubature nodes adaptively.
